What the water could not erase

It happened slowly, then all at once. The sea climbed the steps of every city until the lands were gone, and the roads, and the libraries with them. That was a very long time ago. No one left alive remembers the shore.

But ink is stubborn. The poems are still down there, settled in the silt above the places where they were written, beneath the countries the old maps drew. Recent ones lie in the shallows. Older words have sunk deeper.

You have a boat, a line, and a lure you can weight to any depth. The water is murky, but where you stop to fish it clears, and you may see what still stands below. Sometimes you raise a poem, sometimes a boot.

Whatever you raise is written into your Angler’s journal, which is kept in this browser.

Row

Fish
Space to cast: the first press sets how far, the second sets how deep. Then hold Space to reel when something bites
Look
G shows the globe. Drag to turn it, click a country to sail there

R opens your journal, where every poem you have raised waits to be read again

The sea follows your local time unless you choose otherwise.

Poems first published before 1929 are presented in full, where copyright permits. Those originally written in other languages are rendered into plain English especially for the Library. More recent works are represented by catalogue cards bearing the title and poet’s name, in recognition of copyright and the rights of their creators. Where available, links to Wikipedia offer a creative commons starting point for further exploration of these poems and their authors. The poems themselves remain for you to discover on dry land.

Add a poem to the library

Send a poem of your own, or a favourite, to the Librarian. It may find a home among the books resting on the sea floor of its country of origin. The Drowned Library claims no ownership over the works it receives, nor will it ever. All rights remain with their respective rights holders.

What are you sending?

This site has no server of its own, so the message opens in your email app and nothing is sent until you press send. It goes to librarian@drownedlibrary.org.
